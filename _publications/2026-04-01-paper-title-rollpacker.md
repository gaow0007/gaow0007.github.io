---
title: "RollPacker: Taming Long-Tail Rollouts for RL Post-Training with Tail Batching"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'We propose RollPacker, a system that unifies orchestration of RL post-training components (Actor, Reward, Environment) with fine-grained sample lifecycle control. By batching long-tail samples across training rounds, RollPacker eliminates generation stalls and GPU bubbles, achieving stable end-to-end acceleration and reduced per-step training cost.'
