title: "Weave: Efficient Co-Scheduling for Disaggregated RL Post-Training"
collection: publications
excerpt: 'We propose Weave, a co-scheduling system for disaggregated RL post-training that separates generation and training onto heterogeneous resource pools. Through multi-tenant cross-cluster co-scheduling with execution groups and native async pipeline orchestration at sample granularity, Weave reduces structural wait bubbles and resource mismatch, improving end-to-end throughput and cluster-level cost efficiency.'
